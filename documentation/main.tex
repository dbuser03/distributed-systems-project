\section*{Theme}
WhistleBlower is a blockchain-based platform inspired by WikiLeaks, designed to
empower journalists and whistleblowers to share sensitive information with the
public anonymously and without the risk of government censorship. By leveraging
the immutable nature of blockchain technology, articles published on
WhistleBlower cannot be altered or removed, ensuring transparency and preserving
the integrity of investigative journalism.

\section*{Implementation}

\subsection*{Platform introduction}
Our frontend is an application deployed on the Internet Computer, and the
backend is implemented in Motoko. We use Internet Identity for authentication.

For development we rely on a local Internet Identity canister, while in
production we use the public Internet Identity service.

Whistleblowers can create threads to share their stories, which may include
multiple files. To upload files, users must create an account and log in using
Internet Identity.

We implemented a role-based access control system with three roles: admins,
journalists, and users (whistleblowers). Admins manage and moderate the
platform. Admins nominate journalists, whose main role is to review stories
submitted by users before they are verified; this helps ensure content is
accurate and reliable before publication.

Users (whistleblowers) can create threads and upload files, but stories are not
verified until a journalist reviews and approves them.

Users can filter stories by criteria such as publication status, industry tags,
and country. They can also comment on stories and vote on them.

Each user has a credibility score defined as the ratio of likes to dislikes on
their stories. This score helps estimate a whistleblower's reliability based on
past contributions.
