\section*{Theme}
WhistleBlower is a blockchain-based platform inspired by WikiLeaks,
designed to empower journalists and whistleblowers to share sensitive 
information with the public anonymously and without the risk of government censorship. 
By leveraging the immutable nature of blockchain technology, articles published 
on WhistleBlower cannot be altered or removed, ensuring transparency and preserving the integrity of investigative journalism.

\section*{Implementation}

\subsection*{Platform introduction}
For the frontend, we have an application deployed on the Internet Computer, for the backend We
have used Motoko for its implementation. Moreover, we used Internet Identity for authentication.

Regarding Internet Identity, we rely on a local canister for development while for production we use the public one.

On our platform, whistleblowers can create threads to share their stories, which can include multiple files.

To be able to upload files users have to create an account and log in using Internet Identity. 

We have implemented a role based access control system with three roles: admins, journalists and users (whistleblowers), the idea behind it is that
admins can manage the platform and moderate content, journalists are nominated directly by admins and their main role is to review the stories submitted by users before they are verified.
This is done to ensure that the content is accurate and reliable before being published.

Users (whistleblowers) can create threads and upload files, but their stories won't be verified until a journalist reviews and approves them.

On top of this, users can filter stories based on different criteria such as publication status, industry tags and countries, and most importantly comment on them and 
assign a vote.

Users also have a credibility score, which is a ratio based on the number of likes and dislikes received on their stories.
This allows users to estimate the reliability of a whistleblower based on their past contributions.
